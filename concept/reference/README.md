**Left hand is canon, and it has a reason:** she is right-handed, so she writes on her left hand. Image tools keep putting the writing on whichever hand is visible from behind. Correct it in all final art, where the lettering is added by hand anyway.

**Note on the Refusal:** AI tools turn "leaning back against an invisible pull" into a fighting stance (see `explorations/archive/refusal-02.jpg`). For AI generation, use stillness and a tighter frame. Human artists keep the full-body lean from §5.2.

**These are internal references.** Before any of them is published, check against VISUAL_DIRECTION §2.5. The face must stay out of frame.

## The front view is canonical

Where the views disagree, follow the front view.

- **Darning (Stage 2–3):** four repairs. Ochre on the right upper sleeve, brown on the left elbow, brown on the right pocket, sea blue on the left front hem. The side and back views show extra or shifted patches; treat those as drift. More darns are added only at story points (VISUAL_DIRECTION §6).
- **Headphones:** 1990s over-ear studio headphones. Her **left** cup is taped with silver tape. The coiled cable runs into her **right** cardigan pocket (the scanner).
- **Badge:** clear holder, white backing card, teal lanyard.
- **Inscription:** blue ballpoint *Eu sou a Lua* on the back of her **left** hand.

## Known flaws to correct in future renders

- **Back view:** the inscription text is garbled. Correct spelling: *Eu sou a Lua*.
- **Back view:** the cable crosses over her right shoulder down her back, which doesn't work physically. It should run down her front into the right pocket.
- **Darning style:** the fringed edges are slightly cartoonish. In future renders, prefer flatter woven darning.
- **Hair:** short natural coils, about 3 cm. Accepted as canon for "close-cropped."
