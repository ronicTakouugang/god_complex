# THE GOD COMPLEX

Digital universe for an original fictional superhero: premium graphic novel × classified investigation × cinematic universe.

- **Canon lives in [CHARACTER_BIBLE.md](CHARACTER_BIBLE.md)** (hero, cast, power rules, world; it wins any conflict) **and [STORY_BIBLE.md](STORY_BIBLE.md)** (premise, structure, tone). Read both before writing copy, structure, or visuals. If a decision changes the story, update the bibles in the same change.
- **Visual canon lives in [VISUAL_DIRECTION.md](VISUAL_DIRECTION.md)**: silhouette, palette (with hex values and colour roles), the face rule, motifs, and the imagery guidance for the site.
- Respect FACT / MYSTERY / BELIEF markers in the character bible. Never resolve a MYSTERY by accident.
- Follow the canon rules in §9 of the story bible. In particular: never show the hero's face, always pair a rescue with its cost, and never undo the irreversibility of lost memories.
- Not a conventional superhero landing page. No portrait hero, no bio block, no power cards.
